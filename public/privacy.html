Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

1. Who is responsible

Suvoz (suvoz.io) is operated by HORIZON GATE COMMERCE FZE LLC, Ajman, United Arab Emirates. This policy explains what personal data we collect, why, and what you can do about it. Contact: support@suvoz.io.

2. What we collect

  • Account data: your email address and a password. The password is stored only in scrambled (hashed) form by our login provider; we cannot read it.
  • Usage data: how many characters or minutes you have used, your plan, and the time period of your plan.
  • Payment data: when payments are enabled, they are handled by Stripe. We receive confirmation of payment and a customer reference. We never see or store your full card number.
  • Technical data: your IP address and basic request information, used to keep the service secure and to limit abuse.
  • Your text and audio: the script you write is sent to our voice provider to create the audio. We save a copy of your script and the finished audio in your History, so you can find them again. Only you can see them. They are deleted automatically after 30 days, and you can delete any item yourself at any time.
  • Recordings you upload: if you use speech to text or dubbing, the file you upload is sent to our voice provider to be turned into text or into another language. Speech to text results are shown to you and are not saved by us. Dubbing results (the new audio and its translated text) are saved in your History like other audio.
  • Text you translate: if you use the translation tool, your text is sent to our translation provider and the result is shown to you. We do not save it.
  • Voices you create: if you design a voice or clone a voice from a sample, the sample or description is sent to our voice provider, and the voice is stored with that provider and linked to your account. Only you can use your voices. For voice cloning we also record that you confirmed the right to use the voice, with the date and your IP address. Only upload a voice that is yours or that you have clear permission to use.

3. Why we use it

  • to create your account, log you in and send you emails you need, such as the confirmation link or a password reset;
  • to create the audio you ask for and to count your minutes;
  • to process payments and prevent fraud and abuse;
  • to keep Suvoz secure and working, and to answer your questions.

We do not sell your personal data. We do not use your text to advertise to you.

4. Who else handles your data

We use service providers who process data for us:

  • Supabase – accounts, login, plan records and your saved History (scripts and audio files);
  • Render – hosting of the website and server;
  • Resend – sending account emails;
  • ElevenLabs – turning your text into audio, turning recordings into text, creating and storing voices, and dubbing; your text, recordings and voice samples are sent to them for these purposes, under their own terms and privacy policy;
  • OpenAI – translating text; the text you ask to translate is sent to them for that purpose, under their own terms and privacy policy;
  • Stripe – payments, when enabled.

These providers may be located outside your country, including in the United States and the European Union.

5. Cookies and similar storage

We use your browser's local storage to keep you logged in and to remember your language. We do not use advertising cookies. If we add analytics later, we will update this policy.

6. How long we keep data

We keep your account and usage records while your account is open. Items in your History are deleted automatically after 30 days. Voices you create stay until you delete them. If you ask us to delete your account, we delete or anonymise your data, except records we must keep by law, such as payment records.

7. Your rights

You can ask us to show you the data we hold about you, correct it, or delete it, by writing to support@suvoz.io. We will reply within a reasonable time.

8. Security and children

We use encrypted connections and keep access to data limited, but no system is perfectly secure. Suvoz is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

9. Changes

If we change this policy in an important way, we will tell you by email or in the app.
