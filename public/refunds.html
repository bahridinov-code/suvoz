Refund Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

1. Free trial

Try Suvoz free first. The free trial costs nothing, so there is nothing to refund.

2. Monthly plans

You can cancel at any time. After you cancel, your plan stays active until the end of the period you already paid for, then stops. We do not refund part of a period that has already started, and unused minutes are not refunded.

3. Yearly plans

A yearly plan is paid once a year, and its minutes are given to you every month. You can cancel at any time to stop the next renewal. Your plan then stays active until the end of the year you paid for. We do not refund the unused part of a year.

The exception is the first 7 days: if you write to us within 7 days of buying a yearly plan and you have not used any minutes, we will refund it in full.

4. Renewals and upgrades

If a yearly plan renews and you have not used any minutes since the renewal, write to us within 3 days of the renewal payment and we will refund it in full.

An upgrade is a payment for the new plan, and the same rules apply to it, counted from the day of the upgrade. Minutes carried over from your old plan do not count as used.

5. Other refunds

Write to us within 7 days of a payment and we will refund it in full if:

  • you were charged and could not use the service because of a fault on our side that we could not fix;
  • you were charged twice by mistake;
  • you were charged after cancelling in time.

We may also refund in other cases at our discretion. If the law where you live gives you more rights than this policy, those rights apply.

6. Introductory offers

If you bought a plan with a first-month discount and are refunded, the refund is the amount you actually paid.

7. How to ask

Email support@suvoz.io from the email address of your account, and say what went wrong. Our support team replies within 24 hours. Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method, usually within 5 to 10 business days, depending on your bank.
